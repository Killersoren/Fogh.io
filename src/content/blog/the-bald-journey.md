> "A clear head leads to clear code." – Esben Fogh

## The First Strand Falls (2020)

I still remember the moment I noticed the widening part in my hairline while debugging a stubborn C# routine at VIA University College. Instead of panicking, I laughed, pushed the stray strands aside, and kept coding.

*Lesson:* Elegance in code isn't about appearances—it’s about structure and purpose.

## Embracing the Shine (2021)

By the second semester I ditched the half-hearted comb-over and took a razor to my head.  Suddenly the morning routine dropped from **15 minutes to 30 seconds**.  The extra time?  I invested it in TypeScript and the magical world of functional components.

*Result:* Lighthouse scores up, shampoo costs down.

## Confidence == Productivity (2022)

Going fully bald gave me an unexpected boost in confidence when presenting group projects.  Talking CI/CD pipelines is a lot easier when you’re not worried about a rebellious tuft of hair flopping forward during the demo.

*Highlight:* Led a team that automated coursework deployment with GitHub Actions—scored top marks.

## From Campus to Construction – FL JORD (2025)

Fast-forward to today: as an **RPA Developer** I automate SharePoint workflows and Power Automate flows for the construction industry.  Clients love the clean solutions—and the glare from my dome keeps everyone awake during sprint reviews.

*Key takeaway:* Minimal overhead on your head translates to minimal overhead in your codebase.

## Tips for Fledgling Bald Developers

1. **Polish the USP** – Your bald look is memorable.  Leverage it at networking events.
2. **Sunscreen & Sass** – Protect your scalp and your stylesheets.
3. **Reflect—Literally** – A shiny head is the perfect whiteboard during impromptu architecture discussions.

---

Baldness isn’t a curse; it’s an **optimization**—one that has shaped my journey from student to software engineer.  Here’s to every line of code (and every lost strand) that brought me here.
